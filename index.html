About Me

I am an undergraduate student majoring in Data Science and Big Data Technology at the School of AI and Advanced Computing, Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University, where all teaching is delivered in English. I have hands-on experience across the full data pipeline — collection, cleansing, visualisation and statistical analysis — and I enjoy turning raw data into decisions that hold up.

In the summer of 2026 I worked as a data intern at Shaanxi Yiruibodi Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd., supporting data cleansing, statistical analysis and technical documentation for intelligent-technology projects. As project lead of a university research project (LIF001), I designed questionnaires, collected and cleansed samples, and delivered an analytical report on self-study challenges among university students. I am passionate about AI and energy-industry data applications, and I approach work hardworking, outgoing and eager to learn.
